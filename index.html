    What does the * next to some prices mean?

    Those items can vary depending on size or condition (a huge living room, a very full set of cabinets, an oversized sofa). We'll confirm the final price with you before any work starts.

    How do hourly moving packages work?

    The EZ Move Package is $150/hr with two movers, equipment and a 10 ft truck or van (2-hour minimum). The Commercial Move Package is $200/hr with three movers, equipment and a 26 ft truck (3-hour minimum). Need muscle only? Book movers à la carte from $60/hr and add the $40 equipment fee if you need gear.

    What's the difference between Basic and Deep kitchen cleaning?

    Kitchen (Basic, $60) covers the sink, floors and appliance exteriors. The Deep Kitchen ($200) adds the inside of the fridge, stove and oven, microwave, a dishwasher cleaning cycle, plus emptying and wiping every cabinet and drawer.

    How is storage billed?

    Storage is billed per item, per month — $10 for a standard stackable box, $20 for large items like a mini-fridge, bike, TV or desk chair, $50 for a sofa or bed frame, and $75 for a mattress.

    Can you store my car?

    Possibly! Vehicle storage is by inquiry only. Hit “Ask about it” on the Auto/Car card in the Storage tab and tell us what you've got.
