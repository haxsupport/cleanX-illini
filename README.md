This does not imply endorsement or grant additional rights to third parties.## cleanX adaptationThe original visual shell, responsive pricing panel, service tiles, gradients and chat-card design are retained. cleanX uses Chicago coverage and its own residential rates and monthly commercial contract ranges. The cleanX map is a separate copy of the supplied north/south coverage polygons, with cleanX branding and no Maye's Cleaning reference.Residential bases: under 1,000 sq ft $50 Standard / $80 move-in/out; 1,000–2,000 $100 / $130; 2,001–3,000 $150 / $180. Bedroom and bathroom tier totals are added separately. Deep bundle adds $70 for fridge, stove and cabinets, without double charging. Homes above 3,000 sq ft require a custom quote. See calculate() in index.html and _worker.js for the complete approved rules; keep both copies synchronized.Commercial baseline reference: [ISSA commercial cleaning rates guide](https://www.issa.com/articles/commercial-cleaning-rates-per-square-foot/) (December 4, 2025). Monthly USD per cleanable sq ft: office .09–.17, retail .07–.15, medical .14–.29, industrial .08–.20, schools/education .07–.14. Scope, traffic, condition and frequency affect the final quote; visit count never multiplies the monthly estimate.

cleanX contract terms are billed monthly: month-to-month 0% discount; 3 months 3%; 6 months 5%; 12 months 8%. Owner-approved discounts apply to recurring cleaning only, not specialty projects. A quote request does not sign a contract.

Optional specialty services use their own selected area and separate project estimates: carpet .08–.25/sq ft, strip/refinish .30–.60, scrub/recoat .20–.40, electrostatic disinfection .10–.30. Windows and specialty maintenance plans require a custom quote. Add-ons never silently become monthly charges. Frontend, server validation, AI guidance and quote email summaries share these rules.## Hosting and connectionsCloudflare Pages project cleanx-illini deploys main with no build command and output directory '.'. The root _worker.js uses env.ASSETS for the static site. Add a Workers AI binding named AI. The assistant uses @cf/openai/gpt-oss-20b; no AI API key is exposed in the browser. AI inputs are capped and rate limited per server instance; the calculator remains available if AI is unavailable. Free quotas apply. These in-memory limits are a basic guard, not a durable global quota./api/chat returns a conversational response plus a server-calculated price card. /api/quote-prepare validates contact fields and recomputes the price before returning an email payload. After the customer explicitly confirms the request, the browser posts that payload using FormSubmit AJAX to dani@hax.support, without opening an email application. FormSubmit requires recipient activation. Requests are estimates only; no payment or confirmed booking is created. Final scope, coverage, availability and price are confirmed by cleanX.The browser keeps the current chat in memory only. Contact details are sent to Cloudflare for validation and to FormSubmit for email delivery after consent.
